### Features
- Normal Asteroids
    - Spaceship with movement and physics
        - Momentum decays over time
        - Lose a life when hit
            - Three lives
    - Laser shooting
        - 'spacebar' to shoot laser
    - Destructible asteroids
        - Three sizes, upon laser hit split into smaller
    - Aliens
        - Appear after reaching certain score
        - Shoot at player
        - Killed upon hit
    - Collision
        - Everything collides with each other
    - Gain score for:
        - Time
        - Asteroids destroyed
        - Aliens killed
    - All items warp to opposite side of screen

### Controls
- Screen is 100 by 100
- Ship is 3 wide
- 'w' to add movement in direction of orientation
  - 1m/s^2 acceleration
- 'a' and 'd' to rotate orientation
  - 360deg in 1 sec
- movement decays over time
  - decays 30% per second
  - max of 20m/s
